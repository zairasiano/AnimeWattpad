Coffee and Confessions

By Saki Morino

Two regular customers slowly become each other's favorite part of the day.

Chapter 1: The Usual Order

Every morning, Saki visited a small café near the train station before heading to school. She always ordered a warm latte and sat beside the window to write in her notebook.

One Monday, she noticed a boy sitting at the next table. He wore headphones and spent his time writing on a laptop while drinking black coffee.

The following morning, he was there again. By Friday, Saki had started recognizing his routine. He arrived at eight, ordered the same drink, and always left a few minutes before her.

One rainy morning, Saki reached for the sugar jar at the same time as the boy. Their hands nearly collided, and both quickly pulled away.

"Sorry," he said with a small smile. "You can use it first."

"Thanks. I see you here almost every day," Saki replied before she could stop herself.

The boy laughed softly. His name was Ren, and he was a university student who wrote songs in his free time. Saki admitted that she liked writing short stories, although she rarely showed them to anyone.

After that, they began greeting each other every morning. Sometimes they talked about music, sometimes about school, and sometimes they simply shared the quiet comfort of the café.

One day, Saki found a folded note beside her latte. It read, "I think my mornings have become better since I met you. Would you like to have coffee with me after class?"

She looked toward Ren's table. He was pretending to read his notebook, but his nervous smile gave him away. Saki picked up her pen and wrote her answer on the back of the note.
