My Chaotic Senpai

By Mei Sato

A serious student tries to survive school life with a mischievous senpai who turns every day into chaos.

Chapter 1: The Great Lunch Disaster

Mei had one simple goal for her first week as student council assistant: finish every task, keep everything organized, and avoid trouble.

Unfortunately, her senpai, Riku, had other plans. He greeted her on Monday by placing a toy spider inside her pencil case. Mei screamed so loudly that the entire classroom went silent.

"Excellent morning energy!" Riku announced, writing something on his clipboard. Mei decided that she would never forgive him.

At lunch, Mei carefully carried a tray of rice, soup, and juice toward an empty table. Riku appeared out of nowhere, bowed dramatically, and asked her to witness his newest magic trick.

He tossed a spoon into the air. Mei ducked. Riku stumbled, the juice flew across the room, and the student council president ended up wearing a very orange shirt.

Everyone stared. Riku raised both hands. "Good news! Lunch is now a school-wide event!"

Mei covered her face, but she could not stop laughing. Maybe school life with Riku would be impossible to organize—but it would never be boring.
