Secrets of the Blue Train

By Aya Mizuki

A late-night train ride turns into a puzzle with no easy answers.

Chapter 1: The Midnight Passenger

Aya boarded the Blue Train just before midnight. She was traveling to her grandmother's town and expected a quiet journey through the countryside.

The train was almost empty. Only a few passengers sat in separate carriages, each appearing lost in their own thoughts. Aya chose a window seat and watched the city lights disappear behind her.

A sudden announcement interrupted the silence. "Dear passengers, please remain in your seats. We have encountered an unexpected delay."

Aya checked her phone, but there was no signal. When she looked outside, the train was passing through a stretch of forest she had never seen on the route map.

Curious, she walked toward the next carriage. On an empty seat, she found a small blue envelope with her name written on it.

Inside was a handwritten note: "Find the passenger who boarded at the seventh station. The truth is on this train."

Aya returned to her seat and tried to remember the stations. She was certain she had boarded at the fifth, and the train had stopped only once since then.

Suddenly, the lights flickered. A bell rang from the front carriage, and every passenger turned toward the same closed door. Behind it, someone whispered Aya's name.
