Senpai's Secret Recipe

By Emi Sakura

A cooking club's disastrous recipes become the school's biggest sensation.

Chapter 1: The Explosive Pancakes

Emi joined the cooking club hoping to learn a few simple recipes. Unfortunately, her senpai, Haru, believed every dish needed a dramatic twist.

Their first project was making pancakes for the school festival. Haru added extra sugar, a handful of mystery spices, and something he called his secret ingredient.

The first pancake came out as hard as a book. The second stuck to the ceiling. The third somehow landed on the club president's head.

Emi stared at the kitchen in disbelief while Haru proudly wrote down the recipe. "We may have discovered a new style of cooking!" he announced.

Instead of giving up, the club members turned their failed pancakes into a funny festival attraction. Students lined up to see what ridiculous dish would come next.

By the end of the day, their cooking booth was the most popular one at school. Emi laughed and admitted that cooking with Haru was never going to be ordinary.
