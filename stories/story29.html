The Clockmaker's Puzzle

By Haruka Shin

A broken antique clock holds the answer to a decades-old secret.

Chapter 1: The Clock That Stopped

Haruka loved visiting her grandfather's old clock shop. The tiny store was filled with ticking clocks, wooden shelves, and tools that had been used for generations.

After her grandfather passed away, Haruka discovered a dusty box beneath his workbench. Inside was an antique pocket watch with a cracked glass cover and hands frozen at exactly 11:17.

A folded note lay beneath the watch. In her grandfather's handwriting, it read, "When the clock stops, the truth begins."

Haruka searched the shop for another clue. She examined the shelves and checked the drawers, but nothing seemed connected to the mysterious message.

As she opened the pocket watch, she noticed tiny numbers engraved around its inner cover. They looked random until she arranged them according to the positions of a clock's hands.

The numbers formed an address for an abandoned railway station outside town. Haruka remembered hearing that the station had closed decades ago after an unexplained incident.

She visited the station the following afternoon. Behind a loose brick in the waiting room, she found a small metal key and a photograph of two young men standing beside the same clock shop.

One man was her grandfather. The other had been carefully scratched out of the photograph.

On the back, someone had written a date from thirty years earlier and a final message: "Find the missing clockmaker before the final hour."

Haruka closed her hand around the key. Her grandfather had left her more than an old watch. He had left her a mystery that someone had tried to keep hidden for decades.
