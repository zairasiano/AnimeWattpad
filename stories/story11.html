The Vanishing Diary

By Mio Shira

A missing diary contains clues to a classmate's disappearance.

Chapter 1: The Empty Desk

Mio noticed the empty desk as soon as she entered the classroom. Her classmate, Aki, was usually the first student to arrive, but that morning her seat remained untouched.

At first, everyone assumed Aki was simply absent. But when their teacher asked whether anyone had heard from her, the classroom fell silent. Nobody had seen Aki since the previous afternoon.

During lunch, Mio found a folded note beneath Aki's desk. It contained a short message: "If I disappear, look where our story began."

Mio remembered the old library where she and Aki had first become friends. After classes, she went there and searched the shelves they used to visit together.

Behind a row of dusty books, she discovered a small envelope bearing Aki's name. Inside was a photograph of the two friends and a key tied to a blue ribbon.

The key opened a drawer in the library's reading table. Inside lay Aki's missing diary. Several pages were filled with dates, drawings, and strange notes about a place near the river.

One entry was underlined three times: "The truth is hidden where the old clock stops." Mio looked at the library clock. Its hands had stopped at exactly 4:15.

Suddenly, a new note slipped from the diary. It contained a location and a message in Aki's handwriting: "Mio, if you're reading this, please come alone."

Mio closed the diary and took a deep breath. She did not know where Aki was, but the clues suggested that her disappearance was not an ordinary mystery. Tomorrow, she would follow the trail and discover what Aki had been hiding.
