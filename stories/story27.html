The Last Train Home

By Ren Tsukasa

A passenger realizes nobody else remembers boarding the final train.

Chapter 1: The Empty Station

Ren left his part-time job later than usual. By the time he reached the station, the platform was nearly empty, and the digital clock showed eleven fifty-eight.

Just as he thought he had missed the last train, an old silver train arrived without making a sound. Its doors opened, and a conductor in a dark uniform motioned for him to board.

Ren stepped inside and found a seat beside the window. The carriage was dimly lit, but several passengers sat quietly throughout the train. Nobody spoke or looked at their phones.

After a few minutes, Ren checked the route displayed above the door. None of the station names looked familiar. He asked the passenger beside him where the train was going, but the man only stared ahead.

Ren walked to the conductor and showed him the address saved on his phone. The conductor studied the screen and shook his head.

"This train only takes passengers home," he said. "But you must remember where you boarded."

Ren returned to his seat and tried to recall entering the station. He remembered leaving work, walking along the street, and seeing the train arrive. Yet he could not remember buying a ticket or passing through the gates.

Suddenly, the train slowed down beside a deserted platform. A sign outside read the name of Ren's hometown, but every building beyond it looked unfamiliar.

The doors opened. All the passengers stood up at once and walked out. Ren remained seated until the conductor turned toward him.

"Your stop is coming," the conductor said. "When the doors open, make sure you recognize the way home."
