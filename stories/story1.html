Moonlight Confession

By Aki Hana · Romance

A shy student discovers a secret love beneath the stars.

Chapter 1: Under the Stars

The school courtyard was quiet beneath the silver moon. Sakura stood beside the cherry blossom tree, holding the letter she had rewritten a hundred times.

She had always watched Haruto from a distance. He was the boy who smiled at everyone, yet somehow never noticed the feelings she kept hidden.

"Sakura?" a familiar voice called. She turned and found Haruto standing behind her. Her heart began to race.

"I found your letter," he said softly. "And I was hoping you would tell me what it means."

Sakura looked at the moon, then back at him. For the first time, she decided not to hide. "It means I like you, Haruto."

He smiled, his cheeks turning pink. "Good," he whispered. "Because I like you, too."

Beneath the falling cherry blossoms, a new chapter began—not on paper, but in both their hearts.
