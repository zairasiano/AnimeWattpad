Crimson Blade

By Haru Akane

A young swordswoman must protect her village and uncover the truth behind a legendary red sword.

Chapter 1: The Red Promise

The village of Akatsuki had known peace for many years. Every evening, Haruka trained with her wooden sword beneath the old cherry tree, dreaming of becoming a guardian like her late father.

One morning, the village bell rang three times. Smoke rose beyond the northern hills, and strangers wearing black armor marched toward the village gate.

Haruka rushed to the shrine, where her grandmother kept a sword wrapped in crimson cloth. Its blade shimmered red as soon as Haruka touched its handle.

"This sword chooses its wielder," her grandmother warned. "But its power comes with a promise: never draw it for revenge."

Haruka gripped the sword and ran toward the gate. She was afraid, but behind her stood the people who had raised her. She would not let them face danger alone.

As the first clash of steel echoed through the village, a masked warrior stepped forward and called Haruka by her father's name.
