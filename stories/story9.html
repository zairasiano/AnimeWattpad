Whispers After Midnight

By Rei Kuroda

Mysterious whispers lead a student to a forgotten shrine.

Chapter 1: The Voice in the Dark

Rei had always heard strange stories about the old shrine at the edge of town. According to the other students, nobody visited it after sunset because mysterious voices could sometimes be heard there.

Rei never believed the rumors. To him, the shrine was simply an abandoned place hidden among tall trees. But one evening, while walking home from the library, he heard someone quietly calling his name.

"Rei..."

He stopped and looked around. The street was empty, and the wind moved gently through the trees. Thinking he had imagined it, he continued walking.

Then the whisper came again, this time from the narrow path leading toward the shrine. Curious, Rei followed the sound while keeping his phone flashlight pointed at the ground.

Near the entrance, he discovered a small wooden charm lying beside a stone lantern. Its surface carried a name written in faded ink. When Rei picked it up, the whispers suddenly stopped.

Inside the shrine courtyard, he noticed an old notice covered in dust. It mentioned a student who had disappeared many years ago and whose belongings had never been found.

Rei looked at the wooden charm again. The name written on it matched the name on the notice. Before he could read anything else, a soft voice came from behind him.

"Thank you for finding it."

Rei turned around, but nobody was there. He left the shrine with the charm in his pocket, knowing he had discovered the beginning of a mystery that the town had forgotten.
