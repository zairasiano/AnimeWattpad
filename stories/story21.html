Footsteps in the Attic

By Kento Yoru

A family hears footsteps above them in a house with no attic stairs.

Chapter 1: The Sound Above

When the Yamada family moved into their new house, they were excited to begin a fresh chapter of their lives. The house was old but spacious, with wooden floors and tall windows that overlooked a quiet garden.

On their first night, Kento woke up to the sound of footsteps above his bedroom. The steps were slow and heavy, moving from one side of the ceiling to the other.

He assumed it was his father walking around, but when he checked the hallway, every room was dark and everyone was asleep.

The next morning, Kento told his family what he had heard. His father laughed and explained that old houses often made strange noises. Still, Kento noticed that his mother looked worried.

The footsteps returned the following night. This time, the entire family heard them. They followed the sound through the hallway, searching for a staircase or a hidden door leading upstairs.

But there was no attic entrance anywhere in the house. The ceiling was solid, and the building plans showed only one floor.

Kento examined the old plans again and discovered a small room drawn above the hallway. Unlike the other rooms, it had no label.

That night, the footsteps stopped directly above his bedroom. Then three slow knocks sounded from the ceiling.

A folded piece of paper slipped from a narrow crack near the wall. Kento picked it up and read the faded message: "You have finally come home."
