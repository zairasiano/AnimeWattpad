Blazing Sky Warriors

By Daichi Sora

A rookie pilot joins an elite team defending the skies.

Chapter 1: A Rookie in the Sky

Daichi had dreamed of becoming a pilot since he was a child. Every day, he watched the aircraft flying above his hometown and imagined himself protecting the skies.

After years of training, he finally received an invitation to join the Sky Warriors, an elite team responsible for defending the country from unknown threats.

When Daichi arrived at the airbase, he was amazed by the advanced aircraft lined up inside the hangar. Experienced pilots moved confidently around him, making him feel like a complete beginner.

"You're the new recruit?" asked Captain Reina, the team's leader. "Your skills may be impressive, but flying with this team requires courage, discipline, and trust."

Before Daichi could respond, an alarm sounded throughout the base. An unidentified aircraft had appeared near the northern border and was moving toward a crowded city.

The pilots rushed to their aircraft. Daichi followed Captain Reina into the hangar, his hands trembling slightly as he climbed into the cockpit.

"Stay close to our formation," Reina instructed through the radio. "Follow my lead, and don't panic."

As their aircraft rose into the clouds, Daichi saw a strange red light flashing in the distance. The mission was no longer a training exercise. His first real challenge had begun.
