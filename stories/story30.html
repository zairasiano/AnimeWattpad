Operation: Perfect School Day

By Mei Hoshino

A simple plan for a perfect day turns into a chain of hilarious mishaps.

Chapter 1: The Perfect Plan

Mei Hoshino decided that Monday would be the most perfect school day of her life. She prepared everything the night before: her uniform was ready, her bag was organized, and her favorite lunch was packed.

She even wrote a checklist titled "Operation: Perfect School Day." It included arriving early, getting perfect scores, eating lunch with her friends, and going home without a single problem.

The morning started well until Mei's alarm failed to ring. She woke up late, rushed through breakfast, and accidentally wore two different socks.

Determined not to give up, Mei hurried to the bus stop. Just as she arrived, the bus pulled away. She ran after it for a few steps before realizing that her school ID was still on the kitchen table.

Her older brother drove her to school, but they got stuck behind a delivery truck carrying a mountain of oversized stuffed animals. Mei finally reached the classroom just as the teacher began a surprise quiz.

During the quiz, her pen ran out of ink. Her classmate lent her one, but it was a bright pink pen decorated with a giant plastic flower. Everyone nearby tried not to laugh.

At lunch, Mei opened her carefully packed lunchbox and discovered that her brother had accidentally switched it with his spicy seafood noodles. Her friends laughed so hard that Mei eventually joined them.

By the end of the day, her checklist was full of crossed-out plans and unexpected disasters. Yet she had spent the afternoon laughing with her friends and making memories she knew she would never forget.

Mei looked at her checklist and added one final line: "A perfect day doesn't have to go perfectly."
