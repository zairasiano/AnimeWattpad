Under the Cherry Blossoms

By Nao Fujita

Two strangers meet beneath a cherry tree every spring.

Chapter 1: The Same Tree

Every spring, Airi visited the old cherry tree behind the train station. She liked sitting beneath its branches and sketching the pale pink blossoms.

One afternoon, she found a boy already sitting on her favorite bench. He apologized and moved aside, but the sketchbook in her hands caught his attention.

His name was Sora, and he came to the tree whenever he needed a quiet place to think. Soon, their short greetings turned into long conversations.

They talked about school, music, and the places they hoped to visit. Neither of them noticed how quickly the afternoon passed when they were together.

When the blossoms began to fall, Sora gave Airi a small paper bookmark decorated with a cherry flower. "So you'll remember this spring," he said.

A year later, Airi returned to the same bench. Sora was waiting there with another bookmark and a nervous smile. The tree was blooming again, and so was something neither of them had said aloud.
