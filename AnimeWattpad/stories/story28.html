Guardians of the Crimson Sun

By Toma Akashi

A group of young guardians unites to protect their city from danger.

Chapter 1: The Crimson Signal

Toma lived in Solara, a busy city protected by the Guardians of the Crimson Sun. The guardians were a special team trained to defend the city from mysterious energy storms that appeared without warning.

Although Toma admired them, he never imagined becoming one. He was an ordinary student who spent his afternoons helping at his family's repair shop.

One evening, a deep alarm echoed across the city. A bright crimson symbol appeared in the sky, signaling that an unknown threat had crossed the outer barrier.

Toma hurried outside and saw people gathering in the streets. Above the rooftops, a wave of dark energy moved toward the city center.

A young guardian named Akane landed nearby and ordered everyone to move toward the emergency shelters. Toma noticed that her equipment was damaged, leaving her unable to contact the rest of her team.

Remembering what he had learned while repairing machines, Toma offered to fix her broken communication device. Akane hesitated, but the approaching threat left them little time.

Toma worked quickly and restored the connection. The other guardians soon answered, coordinating their positions across the city.

Akane looked at him with surprise. "You just helped save our entire communication network. What's your name?"

Before Toma could answer, the crimson symbol flashed again. Akane received a message from the team's captain: the barrier was failing, and they needed help immediately.

Toma looked toward the glowing sky. He had always believed heroes were people born with extraordinary powers. Now he understood that courage could begin with an ordinary person choosing to help.
