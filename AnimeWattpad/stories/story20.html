The Witch of Silver Lake

By Shion Yume

A young witch must save a lake whose magic is fading away.

Chapter 1: The Fading Magic

In a village surrounded by forests, a young witch named Liora lived in a small cottage near Silver Lake. The lake was famous for its glowing water, which was believed to hold ancient magic.

Every evening, Liora visited the shore to collect water for her spells. The lake had always shimmered beneath the moon, but lately its light had begun to fade.

One morning, Liora noticed that the flowers around the shore had wilted. The water looked gray, and the familiar magical glow had almost disappeared.

Worried, she opened an old book inherited from her grandmother. Inside, she found a faded drawing of the lake and a warning written beneath it: "When the silver light disappears, the heart of the lake is in danger."

Liora followed the instructions in the book and walked deep into the forest. After hours of searching, she discovered a stone arch covered in glowing symbols.

As she touched the arch, a small blue spirit appeared before her. "The lake's magic is fading because its crystal has been hidden," it explained.

"Where can I find it?" Liora asked.

The spirit pointed toward the tallest mountain. "You must find the crystal before the next full moon. Otherwise, Silver Lake will lose its magic forever."

Liora packed her spellbook and supplies. She was afraid of what lay ahead, but she knew the lake and her village were counting on her. At sunrise, she began her journey.
