The Starlight Kingdom

By Yuki Amane

A young traveler searches for a kingdom hidden among the stars.

Chapter 1: The Map of Stars

Ever since she was young, Yuna had dreamed of finding the Starlight Kingdom. According to the stories told by her grandmother, it was a magical place hidden beyond the northern mountains.

People said that the kingdom only appeared when the brightest star shone directly above the mountains. Many travelers had searched for it, but none had returned with proof that it existed.

On her eighteenth birthday, Yuna discovered an old silver map inside her grandmother's wooden chest. Strange symbols covered the paper, and tiny stars seemed to glow whenever she touched them.

"The kingdom is real," her grandmother whispered. "But finding it requires more than a map. You must learn to trust your heart and the people you meet."

The following morning, Yuna packed her bag and began her journey. She crossed green fields, followed winding rivers, and climbed rocky hills as the silver map guided her north.

At sunset, she met a mysterious boy named Ren. He wore a dark blue cloak and carried a lantern filled with glowing stardust.

Ren studied the map and looked toward the distant mountains. "If you're searching for the Starlight Kingdom, you're heading in the right direction," he said. "But the path ahead is not an ordinary one."

Above them, the first star appeared in the evening sky. Yuna tightened her grip on the map and followed Ren toward the mountains, ready to discover the secret that had been waiting for her.
