The Dragon's Promise

By Kira Tsukino

A village healer discovers an ancient bond with a sleeping dragon.

Chapter 1: The Sleeping Dragon

In a quiet village surrounded by tall mountains, a young healer named Aria spent her days helping people. She knew every herb in the forest and could treat almost any illness.

One afternoon, while searching for a rare flower, Aria discovered a hidden cave behind a waterfall. A strange blue light came from deep inside, making her curious enough to step closer.

At the center of the cave lay an enormous dragon covered in silver scales. Its eyes were closed, and ancient symbols glowed around its body. Aria had heard stories about dragons, but she never believed they were real.

Suddenly, the dragon opened one golden eye. A deep voice echoed inside the cave. "At last, the healer has returned."

Aria stepped back in surprise. "Returned? I've never been here before."

The dragon slowly lifted its head. "Your family made a promise to protect me long ago. Now the time has come for you to discover the truth."

Before Aria could ask another question, the ground began to shake. A dark shadow passed over the mountains, and the dragon's expression turned serious.

"Our promise must be fulfilled," the dragon said. "The kingdom is in danger, and only together can we save it."
