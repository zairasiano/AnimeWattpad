Letters to My First Love

By Hana Aoi

Unsent letters reveal the feelings two childhood friends kept hidden.

Chapter 1: The Letter I Never Sent

Hana had kept a small blue box beneath her bed for years. Inside were dozens of letters addressed to her childhood friend, Haruto. Every letter carried feelings she had never found the courage to say aloud.

Hana and Haruto had been neighbors since they were children. They used to walk to school together, share snacks, and talk about their dreams. But as they grew older, their conversations became shorter, and Hana began to notice that her feelings for him were changing.

One afternoon, Haruto told Hana that his family might move to another city. Hana smiled and wished him good luck, even though she felt sad about the news.

That night, she opened her blue box and wrote one final letter. She wrote about their childhood memories, his silly jokes, and how every ordinary day felt special whenever he was around.

The next morning, Hana placed the letter inside her bag. She promised herself that she would give it to Haruto before he left.

At the school gate, Haruto was waiting for her. "Hana, there's something I want to tell you," he said, holding a folded piece of paper behind his back.

Hana looked at the letter in her own bag and took a deep breath. Maybe she was not the only one who had been keeping a secret.
