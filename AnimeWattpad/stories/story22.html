Rise of the Phoenix Guard

By Akira Tendo

A new recruit discovers the courage to lead an ancient order.

Chapter 1: The New Recruit

Akira arrived at the ancient fortress just before sunrise. The red banners of the Phoenix Guard waved above the stone walls, marking the home of an order that had protected the kingdom for centuries.

He had trained for years to earn a place among the guards. Now that he had finally been accepted, he felt both excited and nervous about proving himself.

Captain Sora greeted the new recruits in the courtyard. "Being part of the Phoenix Guard is not about having the strongest weapon," she explained. "It is about protecting others, even when the path is difficult."

The recruits began their first training exercise. They had to cross a series of wooden platforms while carrying a flag. Akira moved quickly, but he nearly dropped the flag when another recruit lost balance.

Instead of continuing alone, Akira stopped and helped the recruit regain balance. They reached the finish line later than the others, but Captain Sora noticed what he had done.

Before the training could end, a warning bell rang from the watchtower. A messenger rushed into the courtyard with news that a nearby village needed protection.

The senior guards prepared to leave immediately. To everyone's surprise, Captain Sora ordered Akira to accompany them.

"This is your first real mission," she told him. "Remember what you learned today. A true guard never protects the kingdom alone."

Akira tightened his grip on his equipment and followed the team beyond the fortress gates. He was no longer just a recruit. His journey as a guardian had begun.
