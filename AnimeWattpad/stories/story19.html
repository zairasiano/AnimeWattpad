A Song for You

By Rin Ayase

A singer and a shy songwriter find their voices through music.

Chapter 1: The Song in the Hallway

Rin loved writing songs, but she was too shy to share them with anyone. Her notebook was filled with unfinished lyrics and melodies she hummed quietly when she thought nobody was listening.

One afternoon, she stayed late at school to finish an assignment. As she walked past the music room, she heard someone singing a beautiful melody.

The singer was Aoi, a student known for performing at school events. Rin recognized the melody immediately. It was the same tune she had written in her notebook the night before.

Rin peeked through the slightly open door. Aoi was singing her unfinished song, but the lyrics were different. Someone had added words that made the melody feel complete.

"That's a beautiful song," Rin accidentally said aloud.

Aoi turned around in surprise. "Thank you! I found the melody written on a sheet of paper near the piano. Do you know who composed it?"

Rin hesitated before admitting that she had written it. Instead of laughing, Aoi smiled and asked if they could work on the song together.

For the first time, Rin felt that her music might be worth sharing. She opened her notebook and showed Aoi the lyrics she had been hiding. Together, they began creating a song neither of them could have finished alone.
