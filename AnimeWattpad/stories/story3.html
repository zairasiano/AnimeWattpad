The Last Moon Princess

By Yuna Mori

A young princess discovers that the moon holds the secret to saving her fading kingdom.

Chapter 1: The Moon's Last Light

In the kingdom of Lunaria, the moon had always glowed above the castle, protecting the land with its silver light. But one night, its glow began to fade.

Princess Elara watched from her balcony as darkness spread across the sky. The royal advisors whispered that the ancient magic was disappearing, and no one knew how to restore it.

At midnight, a tiny silver bird landed beside her. A glowing mark appeared on its wing, shaped exactly like the birthmark on Elara's wrist.

"The last moon princess must find the lost crystal," the bird said. "Only then can your kingdom see the light again."

Elara took her cloak and the small silver map the bird offered. For the first time, she stepped beyond the castle gates, determined to save her home.

Far away, deep inside the forbidden forest, something watched her approach—and its eyes shone like the moon.
