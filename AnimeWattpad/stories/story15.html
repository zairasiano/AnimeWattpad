The Doll in Room Seven

By Riku Hayami

An old doll changes its position whenever the lights go out.

Chapter 1: The Old Doll

When Mika moved into the old boarding house, she was happy to find a quiet room at an affordable price. The owner gave her the key to Room Seven and reminded her to keep the place clean.

The room looked ordinary except for an old porcelain doll sitting on a wooden shelf. It wore a faded blue dress and had long black hair. Mika thought it was strange, but she decided to ignore it.

That night, Mika turned off the lights and went to sleep. When she woke up the next morning, she noticed that the doll was no longer facing the wall. It was now looking directly at her bed.

Mika assumed she had moved it without remembering. She placed the doll back on the shelf and left for school.

The same thing happened the following night. This time, Mika clearly remembered leaving the doll facing the window. In the morning, it was sitting on her desk, its glass eyes pointed toward her.

Feeling uneasy, Mika asked the boarding house owner about the doll. The owner's smile disappeared when she heard the question.

"You should never touch that doll after midnight," the owner warned. "And whatever happens, do not let it leave Room Seven."

That evening, Mika placed the doll inside a drawer and locked it. Just before midnight, she heard a soft tapping sound coming from the drawer.

Then the lights went out. From the darkness, a small voice whispered, "Why did you hide me?"
