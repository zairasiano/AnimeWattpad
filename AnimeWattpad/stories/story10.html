Shadow of the Ronin

By Takeshi Ren

A wandering swordsman protects a village from a rising threat.

Chapter 1: The Wandering Swordsman

A cold wind swept across the mountain road as Ren, a wandering ronin, approached a small village. His dark cloak was covered in dust, and his sword rested quietly at his side.

Ren had traveled alone for years. He had no master to follow and no home to return to. His only promise was to use his skills to protect people who could not protect themselves.

As he entered the village, he noticed that the streets were strangely empty. Doors were closed, shops were abandoned, and worried faces watched him from behind wooden windows.

An elderly villager finally approached him. "Strangers have been taking our food and supplies every night," the man explained. "Tomorrow, they may return for more."

Ren listened carefully and agreed to help. That evening, he waited near the village gate, watching the moon rise above the rooftops.

Before midnight, several masked figures appeared along the road. They carried torches and moved toward the storage houses. Ren stepped into their path and calmly asked them to leave.

Their leader laughed and ordered his companions forward. Ren drew his sword, ready to defend the village. He moved quickly, blocking their attacks and forcing them away from the houses.

When the final attacker dropped his weapon, the leader warned Ren that a powerful group controlled the surrounding mountains.

Ren sheathed his sword and looked toward the distant peaks. Protecting this village was only the beginning. Somewhere beyond the mountains, a greater threat was waiting.
