The Class President's Secret

By Yui Tanaka

The school's strictest student secretly runs a ridiculous meme account.

Chapter 1: The Anonymous Meme Queen

Yui Tanaka was the strictest class president in school. She reminded everyone about deadlines, kept the classroom spotless, and never allowed anyone to submit homework late without a good reason.

Her classmates thought she was serious all the time. Nobody knew that every evening, Yui became a completely different person online.

Behind an anonymous account called "Homework Survivor," she posted funny memes about school life, surprise quizzes, and teachers who assigned projects over the weekend.

Her account suddenly became popular. Students shared her posts everywhere, laughing at memes that perfectly described their daily struggles.

One Monday morning, their teacher announced a classroom activity: everyone had to guess who ran the school's most popular anonymous meme account.

Yui nearly dropped her notebook. Her best friend, Mika, proudly showed her a new meme from the account. It featured a cartoon class president holding a mountain of unfinished paperwork.

"Whoever makes these memes understands our school perfectly," Mika said. "I wish we knew who it was!"

Yui tried to look serious, but a tiny smile escaped. Unfortunately, her classmate noticed and began looking at her suspiciously.

At lunch, the teacher displayed a new meme on the classroom screen. It showed a student panicking after being asked to identify the anonymous meme creator. Everyone laughed, and Yui realized she had accidentally scheduled the post for the whole school to see.

Her classmates slowly turned toward her. For the first time, the strict class president had no rules, excuses, or announcements ready.
