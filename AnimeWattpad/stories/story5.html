The Last Clue

By Sora Rei

A curious student investigates a mysterious disappearance and discovers a secret hidden inside the school library.

Chapter 1: The Missing Page

Sora loved solving puzzles, especially the strange ones no one else wanted to investigate. One Monday, the school librarian asked for help finding a missing page from an old yearbook.

The page had been removed from the year a student named Akira disappeared. No one talked about Akira, and the student's name was absent from every school record Sora could find.

While searching the library, Sora noticed a loose panel behind a shelf. Inside was a folded note: "The truth is where the clock never moves."

Sora hurried to the abandoned music room. Its clock had stopped at exactly 3:17. Beneath the piano lay a photograph showing Akira standing beside the librarian many years earlier.

On the back of the photograph, someone had written three words: "Ask the principal."

Sora put the photograph away. The mystery was bigger than a missing page—and someone had clearly tried to keep it buried.
