The Garden of Lost Dreams

By Koharu Nami

A mysterious garden helps visitors rediscover forgotten dreams.

Chapter 1: The Hidden Gate

Koharu had forgotten what she wanted to become when she grew up. As a child, she dreamed of painting beautiful pictures, but schoolwork and daily responsibilities slowly pushed that dream aside.

One afternoon, while taking a different route home, she noticed a narrow path covered with tiny white flowers. The path led to an old iron gate hidden behind thick vines.

A wooden sign beside the gate read, "The Garden of Lost Dreams." Curious, Koharu pushed the gate open and stepped inside.

The garden was unlike anything she had ever seen. Glowing flowers surrounded a clear pond, and colorful butterflies moved through the air like floating pieces of light.

Near a stone bench, Koharu found an elderly gardener watering a row of silver roses. He greeted her as if he had been expecting her.

"Everyone who enters this garden has forgotten something important," he said. "What have you lost?"

Koharu thought for a moment. "I don't know anymore. I used to love painting, but I stopped because I thought I wasn't good enough."

The gardener pointed toward a small greenhouse. Inside, Koharu saw paintings hanging on the walls. They showed places she remembered from childhood, including the little house where she used to draw for hours.

On a table lay a blank canvas and a set of old paintbrushes. Koharu picked up a brush, and memories of her childhood returned. She realized that she had not truly lost her dream; she had simply stopped making time for it.

Before she left, the gardener handed her a tiny seed. "Dreams grow when you care for them," he said. "Plant this whenever you are ready to begin again."
