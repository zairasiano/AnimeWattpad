My Roommate Is a Cat

By Momo Hikari

A quiet student's life changes when a mischievous cat moves in.

Chapter 1: The Unexpected Roommate

Haru enjoyed living alone. His small apartment was always quiet, his books were neatly arranged, and his daily routine never changed. Everything was peaceful until one rainy afternoon.

On his way home, Haru heard a tiny meow coming from a cardboard box near the apartment entrance. Inside was a fluffy orange cat looking at him with curious eyes.

"Where did you come from?" Haru asked. The cat answered with another meow before jumping straight into his bag.

Haru tried asking the neighbors if anyone had lost a cat, but nobody recognized it. With no owner in sight, he decided to let the little animal stay for one night.

The next morning, Haru discovered that his socks were scattered across the floor, his notebooks had been pushed off the table, and the cat was sleeping comfortably on his pillow.

"You're going to be a lot of trouble, aren't you?" he sighed. The cat stretched, yawned, and knocked a pen onto the floor.

Later that day, Haru named the cat Mochi. He bought food, a small bed, and a few toys. He promised himself that taking care of Mochi would not change his schedule.

By evening, Mochi had fallen asleep on Haru's lap while he studied. For the first time in a long while, the apartment didn't feel so quiet. Haru smiled, realizing that his peaceful life had become much more interesting.
